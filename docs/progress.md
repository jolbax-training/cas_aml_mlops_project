## 2026-10-30 — Fixed-denominator summaries and run classification (task 3.3 of `add-reviewed-benchmark-and-graders`)

- **Done** (`src/codegen_eval/summary.py`, after rebasing onto the merged run-bundle change). `classify_run` consumes `bundle.verify_bundle` (no completeness logic is repeated) and sorts its problems into `infrastructure` (container unavailable, worker protocol), `exhausted_transport` (`transport_exhausted` trial stop), and `failed_completeness` (missing, tampered, unreadable, wrong-identity or invalid-grade records, and a manifest that is not finalized when nothing else explains it). `run_not_finalized` is implied by any other problem, so it is not counted separately next to an infrastructure or transport stop. A run can carry several classes; any class excludes it from headline comparison.

Missing, tampered, invalid-grade and pending trials stay in the denominator as four unexecuted, unsuccessful checks; a record `verify_bundle` rejected is never read, so it cannot add a success. Stored `incomplete` grades keep their per-check outcomes, so `infrastructure_failures` counts real checks. Reports give counts with rates, trial successes, per-category successes, `trials_incomplete` (infrastructure or no usable record) and `trials_ungraded` (pending grade).
- **Headline eligibility** requires the run-evidence verdict `complete` and no pending grade, and agrees with `RunCounts.complete_for`, which now takes the `Verification`. A bundle with only pending grades keeps the verdict "complete" (every planned record is intact) but is not headline-eligible and its zero successes are not a score.

**Demonstrated scoring bypass in the pilot** (scratch scripts, not committed; unique_in_order case `lists_01_a`): (1) a candidate that mutates its input, then writes a forged `{"status":"returned",...,"args_after":<original>}` to stdout and calls `os._exit(0)` was returned as `status=returned` with clean arguments; (2) a candidate that mutates its input and replaces `json.dumps` to substitute the original arguments was also returned with clean arguments. **Fix:** supervisor (PID 1, non-dumpable) plus separate executor with a per-check token, private report pipe, and pre-bound exact-type serialiser; rationale and limitations in the [threat model](threat_model.md). After the fix the same two candidates give `protocol_violation` and `returned` with `args_after=[[3,1,3,2,1,99]]` (recorded as `mutated_input`).
